5-Week Cut Plan

Goal: lose 4kg in 5–6 weeks (~0.7kg/week — a healthy pace).

The Maths

Maintenance~2,700–2,900 kcal/day
Target~2,150–2,300 kcal/day (~550–600 deficit)
Protein150–170g/day (protects muscle)

Daily Meals

Meal 1
Eggs + salmon + ½ tin tuna (~50g protein) — keep as is
Smoothie
Full protein scoop (~28g) — keep as is
Curry
1 portion + chicken thigh (see upgrade below)
Snacks
30g macadamias (weighed!) + ~80g blueberries (~230 kcal)

#1 rule: weigh the nuts — free-pouring erases the deficit.

Curry Upgrade — Meat

  • Chicken thigh, ~150g raw/portion (+30g protein, ~250 kcal) — moist, flavourful, reheats well
  • Or prawns (lean, quick, suit the coconut base)
  • Brown meat after aromatics, before lentils + liquid

Curry Upgrade — Flavour (low/zero cal)

  • Toast cumin, coriander seed, garam masala with aromatics (biggest win)
  • Bloom turmeric in oil, not liquid
  • Chilli (fresh/flakes) for heat
  • Tomato purée for body + acidity
  • Splash fish sauce/soy for umami
  • Lemon + coriander fresh at the end

→ Puts daily total at ~155–160g protein for ~2,200 kcal
